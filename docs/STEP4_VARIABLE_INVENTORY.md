# Step 4 — Variable Inventory

**Scope:** readiness assessment only. The raw CSV is unchanged; no units were
inferred, values normalized, missing values filled, or rows removed.

## Dataset-wide observations

- `data/raw/tribological_experiments_raw.csv` has 108 records and 25 columns.
- CSV parsing yields text values for every column; the file does not declare
  field types. Numeric parseability below is a separate, non-mutating check.
- The 108-row source manifest contains 108 `VERIFIED_EXACT` rows. Step 3
  compared the raw cells and mapped fields against the indexed DOCX records.
- The exact COF strings `0. 29` and `0. 36` are source-confirmed formatting
  anomalies. They remain unchanged and fail decimal parsing.
- Counts below describe blank CSV cells. A blank may mean a column was absent
  from a source, the source cell was blank, or source context was not copied;
  it is not assumed to be a zero.

## Interpretation notes

1. “Source verified” means the row's transcribed cell text matches its indexed
   DOCX record. It does not establish the paper-to-DOCX relationship, physical
   definition, unit conversion, calibration, or measurement protocol.
2. Candidate input means a field worth evaluating after scientific release
   gates; it does not mean its causal effect or predictive value is known.
3. The `interpreted` filler component fields are not uniformly source-explicit.
   Do not treat them as confirmed formulation inputs until the FeTiO3 split is
   established.
4. Record IDs, sample numbers, raw JSON, source names/indices/labels,
   extraction status, and heading summaries are not physical predictors.
5. The dataset has 12 source tables with nine rows each and 14 repeated
   cross-material measurement signatures affecting 28 rows. Whether they are
   shared controls or repeated transcriptions remains open; they must not be
   deleted automatically.
